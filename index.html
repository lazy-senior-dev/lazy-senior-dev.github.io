lazy-senior-dev: the senior engineers your AI agent can be.

One persona per plugin. Each measured on the thing that engineer cares about.

Give a coding agent a checklist and it skims it. Give it a character and it becomes that character. Each persona here is a different senior engineer with a different job: the reviewer who blocks the merge, the on-call engineer who asks how it fails, the veteran who remembers the last time. They install the same way, share one look, and each is benchmarked on the one number that engineer would put on the wall: defects caught, incidents prevented, outages not repeated.
